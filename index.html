House Blueprint & Technical Utilities

Engineering details of electrical capacity, lighting circuits, ceiling fan compatibility, and heating/cooling infrastructure.

⚡ Entry Switchboard (PDL DBF 15)

Single Phase 40A Supply • AS/NZS 3439.3 Compliant • Located behind front entrance door
✨ 4 Spare DIN Poles Available

The switchboard is configured with a 40A Main Switch (LANMCB6140), a dedicated 20A Wall Oven circuit, two 40A 30mA Legrand RCD Safety Switches protecting split power and lighting circuits, a dedicated 20A Clipsal breaker for the living room split-system AC, and 4 empty DIN rail slots under modular blanks. Multiple Earthed Neutral (MEN) connection and earth electrode are located at the site meter pillar outside.

💡 Ceiling Fans & B22 Oyster Bases

Every bedroom and the living room feature standard surface-mounted flush oyster bases with B22 bayonet fittings. Because modern DC ceiling fans draw only ~30W (0.15A–0.2A) and use wireless remotes, they can replace these existing light points directly onto the joists with zero wall wiring or plaster chasing.

🔦 Kitchen Recessed Downlights

Kitchen and task zones utilize spring-retained recessed ceiling canisters with MR16 / GU5.3 ceramic bi-pin sockets. These can easily be popped down to upgrade to modern integrated tri-colour dimmable LEDs whenever desired.

❄️ Heating & Air Conditioning Strategy

🔥 Brivis Gas Ducted Heating

Circular ceiling discharge vents in all 3 bedrooms, living, and bathroom, connected to the large return-air intake grille in the central entry hallway. Delivers rapid whole-house heating during cold Ballarat winters.

❄️ Reverse-Cycle Split System AC

Wall-mounted split system installed high on the living area courtyard wall, running on its own dedicated 20A Clipsal circuit. Ceiling fans in bedrooms will pull cool air down the hallway to cool the sleeping zones efficiently.
