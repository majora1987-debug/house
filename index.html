Technical Utilities & Infrastructure

Electrical capacity, switchboard pole layout, lighting circuits, and climate hardware specifications.

Entry Switchboard (PDL DBF 15)

Single Phase 40A Supply · AS/NZS 3439.3 Compliant · Behind front entrance door
4 Spare DIN Poles Available

Configured with a 40A Main Switch (LANMCB6140), dedicated 20A Wall Oven circuit, two 40A 30mA Legrand RCD Safety Switches protecting split power and lighting circuits, a dedicated 20A Clipsal breaker for the living room split-system AC, and 4 vacant DIN rail positions under blank covers. Multiple Earthed Neutral (MEN) and main earth electrode reside at the site pillar.

Pole Circuit Identification Rating RCD Protection Scope & Coverage

Ceiling Fans & B22 Oyster Bases

All three bedrooms and the main living area utilize flush surface-mounted bases with standard B22 bayonet fittings. Modern DC ceiling fans draw ~30W (0.15A–0.2A) and operate via wireless remotes, enabling direct replacement of existing light points with zero wall chasing or switch rewiring.

Kitchen Recessed Downlights

Kitchen prep and task areas feature spring-retained recessed canisters with MR16 / GU5.3 bi-pin sockets. These can be modernized with sealed tri-colour dimmable LED modules during future cosmetic updates.

Heating & Air Conditioning Systems

Brivis Gas Ducted Heating

Circular ceiling registers in all three bedrooms, living zone, and bathroom, connected to the central return air intake in the main entry hallway. Sized for cold Ballarat winters.

Reverse-Cycle Split System AC

Wall unit mounted high on the living area courtyard wall, running on dedicated 20A Clipsal breaker (Pole 11). Ceiling fans in bedrooms will circulate airflow down the central corridor.
